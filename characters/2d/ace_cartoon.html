Ace Sterling · cartoon

Skyhaven model sheet · AS-2D-01 · “before” years

Ace is about 30, a dad, a dreamer who builds things. The cartoon keeps his blueprint colours and features: warm brown skin, a short dark side part, big friendly eyes, a broad build, the blue-grey sweater, khaki trousers and brown shoes. He is drawn short and chunky, about 2½ heads tall, with a big head so his face reads on a phone screen.

One outline weight (3 px at sheet scale), round joins, darker than any fill. No inner lines on the face except brows, nose tip and mouth, so lip-sync swaps stay clean.

Head

Short and chunky: about 2½ heads tall, so the head is almost 40 % of his height (realistic Ace is one eighth). Legs are short, hands are simple mitts. Eyes sit on the head’s middle line, 1 eye apart; pupils always keep a white highlight at 10 o’clock.
